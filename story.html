Our story / A quieter corner

Some places ask
nothing of you.

No big occasion. No need to be somewhere else. Just a well-made cup and a chair you want to stay in.

A feeling, before an address.MORI / An independent café concept
Less of a destination.
More of a small daily habit.

The pause was
the starting point.

MORI began as an idea for the kind of café you return to without making a plan. Somewhere the menu fits on a page, the room feels lived in, and the coffee never needs a long explanation.

We imagined the details first: a round table near the window, a place to hang your coat, room for a book beside your cup. Ordinary things, considered properly.

A small menu is a decision.

Espresso with enough character to notice. Milk drinks with a smooth finish. A good matcha for the days when coffee isn’t the answer. Something flaky, something creamy, and no need to overthink it.

There is room for your rhythm.

Some mornings are quiet. Others are full of conversation. The idea is to make space for both — without asking anyone to hurry up and be somewhere else.

MORI is a fictional brand created as a portfolio concept. Photographs are mood references, not images of an operating MORI café.
